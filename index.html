About me

From the physics of the early universe to practical data tools.

I hold a Ph.D. in Physics from National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan, and I am now based in Adelaide. My research has taken me from searches for new particles at the Large Hadron Collider to the formation of primordial black holes in the early universe.

The common thread is quantitative method: building mathematical models, running large Monte Carlo simulations on high-performance computers, and using Bayesian inference and machine learning to draw reliable conclusions from noisy, high-dimensional data.

Alongside research I build interactive tools, from data dashboards and teaching apps to a 3D virtual research campus, because good analysis is most useful when other people can explore it themselves.

Research interests

The questions I keep coming back to, and the methods I use to answer them.

Machine learning for physics data

Using supervised classifiers to separate overlapping signals in large simulated collider datasets, and to tell different sources of new physics apart.

ClassificationFeature engineeringPython

Collider phenomenology

New-physics searches at the LHC, HL-LHC, Belle II, Higgs factories and the Forward Physics Facility, covering axion-like particles, dark photons, sterile neutrinos, leptoquarks and Z′ bosons.

MadGraphPythiaDelphes

Cosmic inflation

Higgs inflation and its extensions, including four-form couplings, and what they predict for the cosmic microwave background and primordial gravitational waves.

Numerical ODEsMathematicaPlanck/BICEP

Primordial black holes

How black holes could form in the early universe, whether they could make up dark matter, and their signatures in gamma rays and as factories of dark particles.

Dark matterGamma raysHawking radiation

Gravitational waves

Stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds from the early universe, and how they relate to pulsar-timing-array signals such as NANOGrav.

PTANANOGravSpectral modelling

Statistical inference

Bayesian inference, MCMC sampling and likelihood analysis to put rigorous constraints on models, with honest uncertainty quantification.

BayesianMCMCMonte Carlo
